# 🎉 Chivdo – Celebrating Love, Culture, and Connection

**Chivdo** is a vibrant, interactive web experience designed to capture the beauty of love, unity, and celebration.  
Originally inspired by the unforgettable wedding of **Davido & Chioma**, this project honors their journey and showcases how technology can bring people together in moments of joy.

---

## 🌟 Features
- **Elegant & Modern Design** – A layout that feels fresh and engaging.
- **Responsive Interface** – Optimized for all devices, from mobile to desktop.
- **Smooth Navigation & Animations** – Experience fluid page transitions and interactive elements.
- **Event Celebration Theme** – Crafted to radiate happiness, romance, and Nigerian cultural pride.

---

## 💌 A Special Tribute
On the occasion of **Davido & Chioma’s wedding**, this project serves as a digital keepsake to:
- Celebrate true love in the spotlight.
- Showcase Nigerian tradition meeting modern flair.
- Inspire others to cherish their own unique moments.

> *"Love is the greatest melody, and your wedding day was a symphony."*

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack
- **HTML5**, **CSS3**, **JavaScript**
- Responsive design techniques for mobile & desktop
- Smooth scrolling & optimized media for a seamless user experience
